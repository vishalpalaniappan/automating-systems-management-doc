\subsection{The Role of Meaning}

Intelligence can be understood as a feedback loop encompassing the abilities to perceive, understand, retain, reason, predict, and act. Meaning plays a central role in this process because it establishes the ability to understand what is perceived and reason about what is retained to predict and act. 

The processes outlined in this document establish meaning in the world of software systems and explains how this is used to automate the development and management of software systems. However, this also enables another abstraction that results in deterministic intelligence. The semantic toolkit becomes the language used by the intelligence to communicate its intentions and understand reality.

The system has the ability to understand what was perceived through its meaning, reason about what was perceived and respond. More importantly, the intentions of its response can be automatically constructed and synthesized. There are multiple levels of reasoning, there is the actual internal consistency of the meaning within the closed semantic world and the intelligence behind the reasoning itself. 

This intelligence is deterministic, meaning, it can explain the conclusion that it arrived at. It is very resource efficient and CLP's ability to retain the narrative history of the reality eliminates redundant computation. The same learning process continues using CLP when reality refutes the assumptions of the closed semantic world. However, the learning can also happen at a higher level of abstraction to enable the reasoning to refine through updated meaning.

Perceiving reality to understand its meaning remains in the realm of probabilistic intelligence. However, once that meaning and the certainty of the meaning is understood, a deterministic process can reason about it. This means that these two forms of intelligence are not mutually exclusive and they excel at different things. When working together, they create a much more effective intelligence. This eliminates hallucinations because the reasoning is traceable and its correctness is inherently known.
