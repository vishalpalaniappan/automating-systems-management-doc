\subsection{The Role of Meaning}

Intelligence can be understood as a feedback loop encompassing the abilities to perceive, understand, retain, reason, predict, and act. Meaning plays a central role in this process because it establishes the ability to understand what is perceived and reason about what is retained to predict and act.
