In this framework, the established meaning drives the automated development and management. To make this possible, the framework provides developers a semantic toolkit to specify the meaning of the world and refine it through feedback from the engine. The intelligence of the system is determined by the reasoning in the meaningful narratives of the world, so as a result, the developer is constructing deterministic intelligence.

However, it is also clear that this framework is agnostic to whether the meaning is specified by a machine or a human. It only cares about the meaning that is established and using that to drive the automation. As a result, there are many interesting applications where a machine can be used to establish the meaning of the world and develop software that can realize its intentions. The complementary tool to this framework is intelligence that can achieve its intentions by establishing the meaning of the world and the reasoning about the meaning through its narratives.
